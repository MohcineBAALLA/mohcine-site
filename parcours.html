ENSIAS Rabat Smart Systems Laboratory CNRST Excellence

Excellence in Moroccan Computer Science & Cybersecurity

Founded on rigorous mathematical foundations from the Mathematical Sciences Baccalauréat and Faculty of Sciences Ben M'sik, Mohcine Baalla joined the prestigious École Nationale Supérieure d'Informatique et d'Analyse des Systèmes (ENSIAS) in Rabat. After graduating with High Honors (Mention Très Bien) in his Master's of Systems & Services Security, he was awarded the nationwide PhD-Associate Scholarship (PASS) to pursue doctoral research addressing global security challenges in the Social Internet of Things.
